## Continuous monitoring
The software bill of materials of every shipped release is scanned against the
OSV vulnerability database daily, for as long as devices run that release. New
findings alert us automatically; this policy's timelines start from that alert
or from an external report, whichever comes first.

## Advisories
Fixed vulnerabilities are published at {{ADVISORY_URL}} once a security update is
available. Each advisory includes, per CRA Annex I Part II (4): a description of
the vulnerability, the affected products and versions, its impact and severity,
and clear remediation steps.
